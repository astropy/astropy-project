### Project Description / Scope of Work
This is a continuation of the Cycle 5 request (#484), which was itself a 1-year resubmission of
the earlier 3-year request (#382). I will continue my maintenance work as maintainer of astroquery
and of the astropy.convolution subpackage. I will also continue contributing small items to other
coordinated packages I am involved in, including regions and reproject, and to affiliated
packages, including pyspeckit and spectral-cube.

Astroquery is frequently the first entry point for users retrieving data from archives and
observatories, so keeping it healthy benefits the whole ecosystem, including users running in
cloud environments and users retrieving spectra.

In 2026, there was a massive uptick in spam PRs, DOS attacks on database
services, etc.  Part of my role is to mitigate the damage from these
AI-generated harms, e.g., by attempting to add resilience to user-facing tools
when primary mirrors are taken down and potentially by adding tools to enable
services to blcok harmful use of astroquery.

#### Project / Work / Deliverables
Role and responsibilities covered by this funding:
- Astroquery: primary maintainer of the spectral line database modules and general
  troubleshooter for modules not supported by observatory or archive staff. I review and curate
  changes to the user interface, coordinate with several observatories (NRAO/ALMA, ESO), and
  provide structural guidance and testing.
- astropy.convolution: maintenance, issue triage, and review.
- Small contributions to regions, reproject, pyspeckit, and spectral-cube as needed.
- Continue to develop and refine astropy-learn tutorials used in university observational
  astronomy courses, incorporating feedback from other faculty who have used them.

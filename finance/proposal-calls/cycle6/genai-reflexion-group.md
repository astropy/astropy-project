### Project Description / Scope of Work
Generative AI use in our community is extremely new and of questionable
sustainability. In particular, a significant fraction of it is, at the time of
writing, effectively subsidised by Anthropic giving away compute to open source
maintainers, and it is unclear what should happen after these allocations are
exhausted.

I propose to dedicate time forming, leading and coordinating a group effort to
define exactly which use cases are acceptable and which are not, focusing on
what can realistically be sustained over the future of the project, with the
hope to avoid forming hard dependencies on a technology of uncertain future.
